About Fempower

Fempower is a women-only community based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, connecting 300+ women across 15+ nationalities. We host mentor walks, peer-to-peer coaching circles, a daily WhatsApp community and in-person events every 15 days across the UAE — from Dubai Marina to Abu Dhabi.

What we offer

  • WhatsApp Community — daily prompts, book club, honest conversations.
  • Mentor Walks — quarterly walking-and-talking cohorts of 10 mentor–mentee pairs.
  • Coaching Circles — peer coaching on negotiation, leadership, personal brand.
  • Events every 15 days — meetups across the UAE, Iftar nights, annual review sessions.
